1. Acceptance

By accessing or using SEN-GEN AI, you agree to these Terms and Conditions and acknowledge the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the system. These terms add to, and do not replace, the student handbook and the policies of STI College Alaminos.

2. About the service

SEN-GEN AI is the system STI College Alaminos uses for student registration through the digital Student Information Sheet, admission requirement tracking, subject enlistment, class scheduling, faculty load management, and related notices and reports. Its "AI" is a rule-based scheduling engine that arranges classes, rooms, and faculty without conflicts. It does not use your personal data to make predictions about you.

3. Accounts and passwords

  • Student accounts are created by the system from your Student Information Sheet. Staff accounts are created by the school's administrators.
  • Change the temporary password you receive the first time you sign in, and keep your password confidential. Turning on two-factor sign-in in your profile is recommended.
  • You are responsible for activity under your account. If you think someone else has used it, change your password and tell the Registrar's Office right away.
  • Do not share an account, and do not use anyone else's.

4. Accurate information

You confirm that the information you provide, including your Student Information Sheet, is true, accurate, and complete, and you will correct it when it changes. False or misleading information may lead to the cancellation of your registration or enlistment and to action under school policy.

5. Enlistment and enrollment

  • You can enlist only after your registration is confirmed and the Admission Office has pre-authorized you. Transferees enlist only after their transfer credits are evaluated.
  • A seat request is not final until the Registrar approves it. Requests can be declined, for example when a section is full or a prerequisite has not been passed.
  • The subjects offered to you follow your program's curriculum, your year level, and your academic record as kept by the school. If any of these look wrong, contact the Registrar's Office.
  • Enlistment is not official enrollment. SEN-GEN AI does not handle tuition, fees, or payments. You complete your enrollment through the school's cashier and its existing procedures.

6. Schedules

Published class schedules may change, for example because of room availability, faculty changes, or other operational needs. When a class you hold a seat in changes, SEN-GEN AI notifies you in the system and by email. Your current schedule is the one shown in the system.
