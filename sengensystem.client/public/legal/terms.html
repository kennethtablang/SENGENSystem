3. About the service

SEN-GEN is the system STI College Alaminos uses for student registration through the digital SIS, admission requirement tracking, returning-student term activation, transfer credit evaluation, subject enlistment, class scheduling, faculty load management, and related notices and reports.

SEN-GEN includes an AI scheduling feature: a rule-based constraint engine that arranges class sections, rooms, and faculty into a weekly timetable without conflicts. It does not use your personal data to make predictions about you, and its output becomes official only when school staff publish it.

The school may add, change, or remove features of the system at any time, for example to follow new school policies or regulations.

4. Who may use SEN-GEN

  • Applicants and students of STI College Alaminos may use it for their own registration and enrollment.
  • Faculty and staff may use it only for the duties assigned to their role.
  • If you are under 18, you confirm that your parent or guardian has reviewed these terms and the Privacy Policy with you and agrees to your use of the system.

5. Accounts and passwords

  • Student accounts are created by the system from your SIS, and the sign-in details are sent to the email address you gave. Staff accounts are created by the school's administrators.
  • You must change the temporary password you receive the first time you sign in. Keep your password confidential and do not reuse one you use elsewhere. Turning on two-factor sign-in in your profile is strongly recommended.
  • After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Your session ends after 8 hours, or earlier when you sign out.
  • You are responsible for activity under your account. If you think someone else has used it, change your password and tell the Registrar's Office right away.
  • Do not share an account, and do not use anyone else's. School staff will never ask for your password.
  • Keep your email address current. A change of sign-in email takes effect only after you confirm it from the new address.

6. Accurate information

You confirm that the information you provide, including your SIS, is true, accurate, and complete, and you will correct it when it changes. You can edit your SIS until the Registrar confirms it; after that, corrections go through the Registrar's Office. The system checks for duplicate registrations, and only one registration per person is allowed. False or misleading information may lead to the cancellation of your registration or enlistment and to action under school policy.

7. Admission requirements

  • The documents required for your program (for example Form 137 or your birth certificate) are submitted in person at the school. SEN-GEN does not accept uploads of them.
  • The system shows a checklist of each requirement and whether it has been received as an original or a photocopy. The checklist reflects what the Admission Office has recorded; keep your own copies and receipts.
  • Missing requirements do not by themselves stop you from enlisting, but you must complete them within the period set by the school. Incomplete requirements may affect your enrollment under school policy.

8. Enlistment and enrollment

  • You can enlist only after your registration is confirmed and the Admission Office has pre-authorized you. Returning students must activate the new term first. Transferees enlist only after their transfer credits are evaluated.
  • The sections offered to you follow your program's curriculum, your year level, your prerequisites, the unit limit for your year level and term, and your academic record as kept by the school. If any of these look wrong, contact the Registrar's Office.
  • A seat request is not final until the Registrar approves it. Requests can be declined, for example when a section is full, a prerequisite has not been passed, or the schedule would conflict. You will be notified of the result, and the Registrar may add a note explaining it.
  • Enlistment opens and closes on dates set by the school. Requests outside those periods are not accepted through the system.
  • Enlistment is not official enrollment. SEN-GEN does not handle tuition, fees, or payments. You complete your enrollment through the school's cashier and its existing procedures. Never pay anyone who claims to collect fees through SEN-GEN.

9. Schedules and the AI scheduling feature

  • Class schedules are generated with the help of the AI scheduling feature and are reviewed and published by authorized staff. A schedule is official only once published.
  • Published schedules may change, for example because of room availability, faculty changes, or other operational needs. When a class you hold a seat in or teach changes, SEN-GEN notifies you in the system and by email.
  • Your current schedule is the one shown in the system. Check it before the start of classes and after any change notice.

10. System-generated documents

Documents produced by SEN-GEN, such as the Certificate of Registration, curriculum prospectus, transfer credit evaluation, and faculty loading confirmation, reflect the school's records at the moment they were generated. They are valid only for the purpose for which the school issues them and only together with the school's official procedures (for example the cashier's validation of your enrollment). Do not alter them. An altered document is void and may lead to disciplinary or legal action.

11. Notifications and emails

By using SEN-GEN you agree to receive service messages from it in the system and by email: account details, sign-in codes, password resets, requirement reminders, approval results, schedule publications, and schedule changes. These messages are part of the enrollment service, so you cannot opt out of them while your account is active. SEN-GEN does not send advertising. Emails from the system never ask for your password; report any message that does.
