Privacy Policy

Version 2 · Effective November 23, 2026 · Applies to SEN-GEN, the online enrollment and class scheduling system of STI College Alaminos

In short: STI College Alaminos collects the information on your Student Information Sheet and the records of your enrollment so it can admit you, enlist you in classes, build schedules, and contact you about your enrollment. Only school staff whose work requires it can see your records. Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to profile you. The system's AI scheduling feature works on class sections, rooms, and faculty, not on your personal data. You can ask to see, correct, or object to the processing of your data at any time.

This policy explains how your personal data is handled in SEN-GEN, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Please read it together with the Terms and Conditions.

1. Who is responsible for your data

STI College Alaminos, Alaminos City, Pangasinan, is the personal information controller for data processed in SEN-GEN. It decides what is collected and why, and it answers for how the data is handled. The school has designated a Data Protection Officer (DPO) who oversees compliance with the Data Privacy Act and handles your privacy requests (see Contact us).

2. Whom this policy covers

This policy applies to everyone whose personal data is in SEN-GEN:

  • applicants and students, including new students, transferees, and returning students who activate a new term;
  • parents and guardians whose names and contact numbers appear on a Student Information Sheet;
  • faculty and staff who use the system or are assigned classes in it; and
  • survey respondents invited to evaluate the system.

It covers the SEN-GEN website, the emails it sends, and the documents and reports it produces. It does not cover the school's other systems (for example its cashier, library, or separate student-records system), which have their own policies.

3. What we collect

From your Student Information Sheet (SIS)

  • Identity: last, first, and middle name; date and place of birth; sex; civil status; citizenship.
  • Contact: email address, mobile number, and permanent address (house and street, barangay, city or municipality, province, ZIP code).
  • Family: names and mobile numbers of your father, mother, and guardian, and your relationship to the guardian.
  • Education: your last school, its program, school year, the grade or year and term you last attended, the program you are enrolling in, whether you are a new student or a transferee, and who referred you, if anyone.
  • The date and time you agreed to the terms when you submitted the form.

Your age, civil status, and educational background are sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act. We collect them only because enrollment requires them and protect them accordingly.

Created during your enrollment

  • Your registration number, official student number, year level, and the status of your registration, term activation, and pre-authorization.
  • Your admission requirements checklist: whether each document (for example Form 137 or your birth certificate) has been submitted, and in what form (original or photocopy). You hand the documents in on campus. SEN-GEN records only their status and does not store copies or scans of them.
  • For transferees, the credit evaluation: which subjects were credited and the subject and grade they were credited from.
  • Your academic record in this system: whether each subject you took was passed, failed, or dropped. Grade values themselves are kept by the school's separate student-records system.
  • Your subject enlistment: the class sections you requested, approved, dropped, or cancelled, the Registrar's notes on those requests, and your resulting class schedule and Certificate of Registration.

From your use of the system

  • Account data: your sign-in email, your password (stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash, never readable, not even by administrators), whether two-factor sign-in is on, failed sign-in counts, and any temporary lockout.
  • Activity log (audit trail): security- and record-related actions, such as sign-ins, failed sign-ins, changes to records, and approvals, with the date and time, the account that performed them, and the IP address they came from.
  • Notifications sent to you in the system and by email, and whether you have read them.

Faculty and staff

Name, email, role, employee ID, program, teaching load and maximum load, preferred teaching times, and class assignments.

Evaluation survey (only if you are invited)

If you are invited to rate the system, your answers are stored with the details you give on the form: name, email, role, position, age, sex, department, and how long you have used the system, together with any suggestions and comments you write. Participation is voluntary.

What we do not collect

SEN-GEN does not collect payment or bank details, government ID numbers, photographs, biometrics, your precise location, or health records. It does not use tracking or advertising cookies and does not track you across other websites.

4. Where your data comes from

  • From you, when you fill in the SIS, update your profile, request class sections, or answer the survey.
  • From school staff, when they record your submitted documents, assign your student number, evaluate your transfer credits, or approve your requests.
  • From the school's existing records, when the Registrar imports a list of prospective or continuing students, or academic results, from a spreadsheet so that you do not need to re-enter them.
  • From the system itself, which records sign-ins and changes in the activity log.

5. Why we process it

  • To register and admit you, check for duplicate registrations, verify your admission requirements, and assign your student number and year level.
  • To evaluate transfer credits and determine the subjects you still need to take.
  • To enlist you in classes: checking prerequisites, seat availability, unit limits, and schedule conflicts, and issuing your Certificate of Registration.
  • To generate and publish class schedules and manage faculty teaching loads and room use.
  • To contact you about your account and enrollment: account details, password resets, sign-in codes, document reminders, approvals, and schedule changes.
  • To keep the system secure, prevent misuse, and hold every change accountable through the activity log.
  • To produce reports for the school's academic planning, such as enrollment counts, room utilization, and faculty load. Reports for planning use totals, not individual profiles, wherever possible.
  • For invited respondents only: to evaluate the system's quality under ISO/IEC 25010. Results are reported in aggregate.

We use your data only for these purposes. If the school ever needs to use it for a new purpose that is not compatible with them, it will tell you first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.

6. Legal basis

We process your personal information because it is necessary to provide the education services you applied or enrolled for (Section 12(b) of the Data Privacy Act) and to comply with the school's legal obligations, including the regulations of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) (Section 12(c)). Security logging and planning reports are done for the school's legitimate interest in running a secure, accountable enrollment system (Section 12(f)).

Sensitive personal information, such as your age, civil status, and education, is processed because it is required by the laws and regulations that govern school admission and records (Section 13(b)) and with the consent you give when you submit your SIS (Section 13(a)). The evaluation survey relies on your consent only. You may withdraw consent at any time, but this does not affect processing already done, or processing the school must still do by law.

7. AI scheduling and automated processing

SEN-GEN includes an AI scheduling feature: a rule-based constraint engine that arranges class sections into time slots, rooms, and faculty without conflicts. It works on curriculum, section, room, and faculty availability data. It does not read your SIS details, does not learn from your personal data, and does not build a profile of you.

  • Generated schedules are reviewed and published by school staff; nothing the engine produces becomes official until a person publishes it.
  • When you enlist, the system checks rules automatically (prerequisites, seat limits, unit caps, and time conflicts) to show which sections you can request. These checks apply the school's published rules the same way for everyone.
  • Whether you get a seat is decided by the Registrar, not by the system. If you believe a rule was applied wrongly to you, you may ask the Registrar's Office to review it.

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you without human review. SEN-GEN does not send your personal data to outside AI services.

9. When we share it

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and we do not share it with other organizations for their own marketing. We share it only:

  • with the service providers who run the system on the school's behalf;
  • with government agencies such as CHED and TESDA when their regulations require enrollment reports or records;
  • when required by law, such as a lawful order from a court, a subpoena, or a request from a government agency with authority to make it; and
  • when necessary to protect your life or health, or someone else's, in an emergency.

10. Service providers and transfers abroad

Two providers handle data on the school's behalf, under its instructions and only for the purpose of running SEN-GEN:

  • SmarterASP.NET hosts the website and its database. Its servers may be located outside the Philippines.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the system's emails to you, such as account details, sign-in codes, and enrollment notices.

Where data is stored or processed outside the Philippines, the school remains responsible for it under the Data Privacy Act and uses providers that protect it with security measures comparable to those described in this policy. The website's fonts and scripts are served from SEN-GEN itself, so simply visiting a page does not send your information to other websites.

11. What is stored in your browser

SEN-GEN does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It keeps a few items in your browser's local storage so the site works:

  • your sign-in session, which expires after 8 hours and is removed when you sign out;
  • your display preferences, such as theme, density, time format, and your chosen start page;
  • a record that you agreed to these documents and which version, so you are not asked on every visit.

These items stay on your device and are not used to track you. You can clear them at any time through your browser's settings. On a shared or public computer, always sign out when you finish.

12. How we protect it

The school applies organizational, physical, and technical security measures, including:

  • All connections to the website and between the website and its database are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes. New student accounts start with a temporary password that must be changed at the first sign-in.
  • Role-based access limits who can see each record, and optional two-factor sign-in (a one-time code sent to your email, valid for 10 minutes) is available for every account.
  • After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes, and sign-in and public forms are rate-limited to resist password guessing and abuse.
  • Password-reset links are single-use and expire after 60 minutes. A change of sign-in email takes effect only after you confirm it from the new address within 24 hours.
  • Record changes are written to an activity log, so every change can be traced to an account, a time, and an IP address.
  • Staff receive access only for their role and are reminded of their confidentiality duties. Exported spreadsheets and printed reports are handled under the same duties.

No system is perfectly secure. You help protect your data by keeping your password private, turning on two-factor sign-in, and signing out on shared devices.

13. If a breach happens

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at real risk of serious harm, the school will notify the National Privacy Commission and the affected persons within 72 hours of learning of it, as required by NPC rules. The notice will describe what happened, what data was involved, what the school is doing about it, and what you can do to protect yourself.

15. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed (this policy);
  • Access your data and obtain a copy of it. Much of it, such as your SIS, schedule, and Certificate of Registration, you can already view in your account;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data. You can correct your own SIS until the Registrar confirms it, and after that through the Registrar's Office;
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it;
  • Have your data erased or blocked when it is no longer necessary, is outdated, or was processed unlawfully;
  • Data portability: receive your data in a commonly used electronic format;
  • Be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your data; and
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Some requests may be limited where the school must keep a record by law, for example official enrollment and academic records. If so, we will explain why.

16. How to exercise your rights

  1. Send your request to the Data Protection Officer through the Registrar's Office (see Contact us). Say which right you want to exercise and which records it concerns.
  2. We will verify your identity before acting, for example by asking you to present a valid school or government ID, so that no one else can obtain or change your data.
  3. We will respond within fifteen (15) working days. If we need more time for a complex request, we will tell you why and when to expect the answer.
  4. Requests are free of charge. A parent or guardian, or a person you authorize in writing, may make a request on your behalf.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may bring your concern to the National Privacy Commission.

17. Information about other people

When you list your parents or guardian on your SIS, you confirm that you are allowed to share their names and contact numbers with the school and that you will let them know it was shared and why. The school uses this information only to contact them about your enrollment or in an emergency.

18. Students under 18

If you are under 18, your parent or guardian should review this policy and the Terms and Conditions with you before you submit your SIS. The guardian information on your SIS lets the school reach them when necessary, and they may exercise your privacy rights on your behalf.

19. Changes to this policy

The school may update this policy, for example when the system or the law changes. The version and effective date above will be updated, and you will be asked to agree again the next time you use SEN-GEN. Important changes will also be announced in the system.
