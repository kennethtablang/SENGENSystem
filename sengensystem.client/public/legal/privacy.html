Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · Applies to SEN-GEN AI, the online enrollment and class scheduling system of STI College Alaminos

In short: STI College Alaminos collects the information on your Student Information Sheet and the records of your enrollment so it can admit you, enlist you in classes, build schedules, and contact you about your enrollment. Only school staff whose work requires it can see your records. Your data is not sold or used for advertising. You can ask to see, correct, or object to the processing of your data at any time.

This policy explains how your personal data is handled in SEN-GEN AI, in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

1. Who is responsible for your data

STI College Alaminos is the personal information controller for data processed in SEN-GEN AI. It decides what is collected and why, and it answers for how the data is handled.

2. What we collect

From your Student Information Sheet (SIS)

  • Identity: last, first, and middle name; date and place of birth; sex; civil status; citizenship.
  • Contact: email address, mobile number, and permanent address (house and street, barangay, city or municipality, province, ZIP code).
  • Family: names and mobile numbers of your father, mother, and guardian, and your relationship to the guardian.
  • Education: your last school, its program, school year, the grade or year and term you last attended, the program you are enrolling in, whether you are a new student or a transferee, and who referred you, if anyone.
  • The date and time you agreed to the terms when you submitted the form.

Created during your enrollment

  • Your registration number, official student number, year level, and the status of your registration and pre-authorization.
  • Your admission requirements checklist: whether each document (for example Form 137 or your birth certificate) has been submitted, and in what form. SEN-GEN AI records only the status. It does not store copies of the documents.
  • For transferees, the credit evaluation: which subjects were credited and the subject and grade they were credited from.
  • Your academic record in this system: whether each subject you took was passed, failed, or dropped. Grade values themselves are kept by the school's separate student-records system.
  • Your subject enlistment: the class sections you requested, approved, dropped, or cancelled, and your resulting class schedule and Certificate of Registration.

From your use of the system

  • Account data: your sign-in email, your password (stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash, never readable), and your two-factor authentication setting.
  • Activity log (audit trail): security- and record-related actions, such as sign-ins, failed sign-ins, changes to records, and approvals, with the date and time, the account that performed them, and the IP address they came from.
  • Notifications sent to you in the system and by email.

Staff and faculty

For school personnel: name, email, role, employee ID, teaching load, preferred teaching times, and class assignments.

Evaluation survey (only if you are invited)

If you are invited to rate the system, your answers are stored with the details you give on the form: name, email, role, position, age, sex, department, and how long you have used the system. Participation is voluntary.

3. Why we process it

  • To register and admit you, verify your admission requirements, and assign your student number and year level.
  • To evaluate transfer credits and determine the subjects you still need to take.
  • To enlist you in classes: checking prerequisites, seat availability, and schedule conflicts, and issuing your Certificate of Registration.
  • To generate and publish class schedules and manage faculty teaching loads and room use.
  • To contact you about your account and enrollment: account details, password resets, sign-in codes, document reminders, approvals, and schedule changes.
  • To keep the system secure and hold every change accountable through the activity log.
  • To produce reports for the school's academic planning, such as enrollment counts, room utilization, and faculty load.
  • For invited respondents only: to evaluate the system's quality under ISO/IEC 25010. Results are reported in aggregate.

Your data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for automated decisions that produce legal effects on you without human review. Schedule generation assigns rooms, times, and faculty to class sections, not to individual students. Enlistment requests are decided by the Registrar.

4. Legal basis

We process your data because it is necessary to provide the education services you enrolled for and to comply with the school's obligations under the regulations of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA). Processing is also done for the school's legitimate interest in running a secure, accountable enrollment system, and with your consent where you give it, such as when you answer the evaluation survey.

5. Who can see it

Access is limited by role. Each person sees only what their work requires:

WhoWhat they can access
You (student)Your own SIS, requirements checklist, enlistment, schedule, and Certificate of Registration
Admission OfficerRegistrations, requirement checklists, student numbers, term activations, pre-authorization

FacultyTheir own teaching schedule and the number of students in each class
School and system administratorsAll records, for administration, support, and the activity log

We do not share your data with other organizations, except with the service providers below and when the law requires it (for example a lawful order from a court or a government agency).

6. Service providers

Two providers handle data on the school's behalf, under its instructions:

  • SmarterASP.NET hosts the website and its database. Its servers may be located outside the Philippines.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the system's emails to you, such as account details, sign-in codes, and enrollment notices.

7. What is stored in your browser

SEN-GEN AI does not use tracking or advertising cookies. It keeps a few items in your browser's local storage so the site works:

  • your sign-in session, removed when you sign out;
  • your display preferences, such as theme, density, and time format;
  • a record that you agreed to these terms, so you are not asked on every visit.

You can clear these at any time through your browser's settings. On a shared or public computer, always sign out when you finish.

8. How we protect it

  • All connections to the website and between the website and its database are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes.
  • Role-based access limits who can see each record, and optional two-factor sign-in is available for every account.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down to resist password guessing.
  • Record changes are written to an activity log, so every change can be traced.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put you at risk, the school will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then in line with STI College Alaminos' records retention policy and the retention periods required by CHED, TESDA, and other applicable regulations. Enrollment and academic records are school records and may be kept after you leave the school. Data that is no longer needed is securely deleted or anonymized.

10. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed (this policy);
  • Access your data and obtain a copy of it;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data. You can correct your own SIS until the Registrar confirms it, and after that through the Registrar;
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it;
  • Have your data erased or blocked when it is no longer necessary or was processed unlawfully;
  • Data portability: receive your data in a commonly used electronic format;
  • Be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your data; and
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Some requests may be limited where the school must keep a record by law, for example official enrollment records. If so, we will explain why.

11. Students under 18

If you are under 18, your parent or guardian should review this policy with you. The guardian information on your SIS lets the school reach them when necessary.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and you will be asked to agree again the next time you use SEN-GEN AI.

13. Contact us

For questions, requests to exercise your rights, or privacy concerns, contact the Data Protection Officer of STI College Alaminos through the Registrar's Office on campus. Please include your name and student number so we can find your records.
